Custom RLA hardware / prototype

The control layer onboard.

The RLA Drive Module is the custom hardware and software concept being developed to connect RaceLab Arena's Command Link to the physical RC vehicle.

Prototype statusNot available for purchase
01 Product concept

Purpose-built for RaceLab Arena

A bridge between command and movement.

The Drive Module is being designed to support onboard steering and throttle integration, command communication, status feedback and telemetry capability.

Public information stays at the product level. Confidential schematics, source code, PCB trace routing, credentials and pin mappings are not published.
