02 / The work

Describe the work at a safe level.

Discuss goals, methods and public-facing observations. Do not include credentials, source code, private network data, confidential schematics, PCB trace routing or pin mappings.

03 / What we learned

Separate observation from conclusion.

Record what the prototype demonstrated, what remains uncertain and which assumptions need more testing.

04 / Next steps

Explain the next validation stage.

Use clear future language. Do not publish invented dates or imply a launch milestone is complete before it is verified.
