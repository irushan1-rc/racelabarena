Prototype in developmentPerth, Western Australia
01 Why RLA exists

The game is digital. The racing is physical.

Put real interaction back into modern gaming.

Simulation has made racing more accessible and more precise. RaceLab Arena takes the next step: translating those familiar controls into movement on a physical track.

The goal is not to imitate driving a full-sized car. It is to create a new physical gaming experience where a real RC vehicle, a live onboard view and measurable track performance come together.

03 Honest development

Prototype before promises

Built in stages. Shared honestly.

The system is still being developed. Work spans simulator input, vehicle control, FPV, telemetry, lap timing and the physical track environment.

There is no confirmed public venue, price list, launch date or booking schedule. Those details will follow prototype testing and experience validation—not marketing guesses.

04 Long-term direction

A platform for physical competition.

The long-term vision is a flexible RLA system supporting different vehicles, driver profiles, challenge formats and shared racing experiences.

  1. 01

    Prove the core link

    Build dependable control between the Race Console and a real physical RC vehicle.

  2. 02

    Validate the experience

    Test FPV driving, driver modes, telemetry, timing and physical challenges.

  3. 03

    Learn through demos

    Use future controlled demonstrations to understand the experience before public launch.

  4. 04

    Grow responsibly

    Explore multi-vehicle racing, partnerships and events only after the foundation is ready.
