Product details

About this bag

Lightweight, rugged and built for the driven. Whether you're heading to the track, the gym, or the weekend away, the RLA Stay Driven Duffel keeps your gear organised. Clean white canvas, bold RLA branding and contrasting black straps.

A roomy main compartment handles clothes or car parts, while an internal zip pocket and exterior mesh pocket keep smaller items reachable.

What's inside the design

  • 100% polyester Oxford canvas — durable and quick-drying
  • Zippered main compartment with black double zipper
  • Internal zip pocket with sewn-in name tag
  • Exterior mesh side pocket for quick-access items
  • Removable, adjustable 1.5-inch shoulder strap with pad and brushed nickel hardware
  • Reinforced carrying handles for heavier loads
  • Available in Small and Large — assembled in AU (±0.5-inch tolerance)

Care: Dry clean only.
