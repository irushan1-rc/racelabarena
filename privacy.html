Starter privacy notice

Privacy in plain language.

This notice explains the current website data flows. It is a development-stage starter document and should receive professional legal review.

This starter wording is provided for transparency during development and is not a substitute for professional legal advice.

01

Information RaceLab Arena may collect.

Information is collected when you choose to submit a waitlist, contact, partnership or merchandise-update form. Depending on the form, this may include your name, email, optional phone number, organisation, interest type, participant count and message.

02

Waitlist and contact forms.

Website forms use Formspree to receive submissions. Information you submit is sent to Formspree and made available to RaceLab Arena so the enquiry can be answered, interest can be recorded or relevant updates can be sent. Do not submit sensitive information through general website forms.

03

External services.

Formspree processes website form submissions under its own privacy terms. Social-media links lead to external platforms that apply their own privacy policies when you visit them.

04

Cookies and analytics.

This website does not currently include a first-party analytics platform or advertising tracker in the public page code. External services may use their own necessary cookies when you visit or use them. This notice should be updated if analytics or additional tracking is introduced.

05

Your choices.

You may ask RaceLab Arena to correct or remove information previously submitted through the website, subject to any information that must be retained for legitimate or legal purposes. You can also ask not to receive future project updates.

06

Privacy contact.

Contact info@racelabarena.com with privacy questions or information requests. RaceLab Arena (ABN 90 706 045 051) is based in Perth, Western Australia.
