01 The concept

The game is digital. The racing is physical.

Not another racing game.
A real machine under your control.

RaceLab Arena connects professional simulator controls to real physical RC vehicles. You sit at the Race Console, drive through a live onboard camera feed and feel every input translate into movement on a physical track.

Custom hardware and software bring together control, video, telemetry, driver profiles and lap timing as one engineered racing experience.

02 System architecture

How it works

Your inputs move through the RaceLab Arena control system to a real RC vehicle. Live FPV and track data close the loop.

  1. 01 Drive input

    Race Console

    Steering, throttle and braking begin at a professional simulator rig.

    Signal out
  2. 02 Command link

    Control System

    Custom RLA technology interprets the drive input and creates the vehicle command.

    Command out
  3. 03 Vehicle link

    Physical RC Vehicle

    The real vehicle responds on track — acceleration, braking and steering included.

    Data return
  4. 04 Track system

    FPV + Telemetry

    Live vision, driver data and lap timing return to the Race Console.

    Loop complete
